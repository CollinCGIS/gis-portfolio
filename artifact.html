      GIS Tools

      Small utilities that run in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Coordinate and distance results were checked against pyproj.

      Coordinate converter

      Latitude and longitude to UTM, Web Mercator and degrees-minutes-seconds, or UTM back to latitude and longitude. WGS84.

      Accepts 40.7128, 40°42'46"N or 40 42 46 N. Zones follow the standard 6° grid and ignore the Norway and Svalbard exceptions.

      Distance and bearing

      Great-circle distance, initial bearing and midpoint between two points.

      Uses a sphere with a 6,371 km radius, which is within about 0.5% of ellipsoidal distance.

      Spectral index calculator

      Enter surface reflectance values (0 to 1) for Landsat 8/9 bands.

      Landsat Collection 2 scaling

      Convert a raw Level 2 pixel value (DN) to reflectance or surface temperature.

      Map scale

      Work out ground distance from a printed scale, or the scale from a measured distance.

      Slope units

      Convert between degrees, percent grade and rise-to-run.

      GeoJSON inspector

      Paste GeoJSON or open a file. It checks the structure, counts features and vertices, measures area and length, and draws a preview.
